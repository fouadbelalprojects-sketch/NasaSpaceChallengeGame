# Arcadia Subsist — Godot 4.7.2

A 2D side-view Mars survival game made in GDScript for Godot 4.7.2.
A lone engineer lands in Arcadia Planitia and has to turn a crash site into a colony that can sustain itself, over five acts.

## Open the project

1. Extract this folder from the ZIP.
2. Open Godot 4.7.2.
3. In Project Manager, choose **Import** and select `project.godot`.
4. Open the project and press **F5**.

## Visuals

- Pixel-art terrain atlas (`assets/terrain_atlas.png`, generated by a script) with layered strata, back walls in caves and mined holes, and soft edge shading.
- Dynamic 2D lighting: a helmet headlamp, glowing dome, lander, underground base, fuel cell, crystals and ladder lamps. Caves stay dark until you light them.
- Separate sky layer with a moving sun (and the blue glow of a Martian sunset), stars and Phobos at night.
- Animated engineer with a walk cycle, climbing pose, mining laser and footstep dust. Mining bursts take the color of the rock you cut.
- Dust-storm wind and haze, and a flickering solar flare.
- Restyled HUD: vitals with critical warnings, a sol clock showing the day/night cycle, cargo list, objective card with ticked requirements, keycap prompts, alerts and act banners. The screen edges flash red when a vital stat is critical (purple for radiation).

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| A / D or Left / Right | Move |
| W / S or Up / Down | Climb ladders (access shaft, deep drill shaft) |
| Space | Jump (also jumps off a ladder) |
| Mouse + hold F | Mine a nearby tile |
| E | Interact: salvage, survey stakes, discoveries, exploration scans, dome machines, wipe panels |
| Hold R | Build the current objective at its site |
| Z | Rest until dawn (inside the lander, the sealed dome or the underground base) |
| Q | Eat (from Act III) |
| T | Recall beacon: return to your nearest shelter (25 s cooldown, costs 8 power) |
| H | Toggle suit heater |
| Esc | Pause / resume |

## Sols (day and night)

- One sol lasts 4 real minutes: dawn, daylight, dusk, then night.
- At night it's dark, the suit heater can't keep up outside, and solar panels produce nothing.
- You can rest (Z) in a shelter after dusk, or any time your next task has to wait for tomorrow. Resting skips to dawn and restores heat and oxygen.

## The five acts

### Act I — The Landing
1. **Salvage the lander** (E): metal and control boards.
2. **Secure the landing zone**: plant beacons at the three orange survey stakes to confirm the ground is stable. Dome construction is locked until this is done.
3. **Build the dome over three sols, one third per sol:**
   - **Sol 1**: foundation and **oxygen generator** (it starts once the dome is sealed).
   - **Sol 2**: **finish the structure**, the pressure shell.
   - **Sol 3**: **temporary energy source**, an emergency fuel cell that runs on water ice from the cavern.
4. **Hide in the spaceship you came in** (Z in the lander). This completes the landing.
5. Next sol, seal the dome (E) and **survive your first sol under it**: work through the day, then rest in the dome after dusk. **Act II starts** when you wake up.

### Act II — Renewable Power
- **Set up the solar panels** (hold R at the marked frame). Dust builds up on bare panels; press E to wipe them.
- **Research points (RP)** come online:
  - **Discovery** (glowing beacons, E): **+10 RP**
  - **Exploration scan** (orange flags, E, then stay inside the zone for 6 s): **+5 RP**
  - **Mining**, by rarity: regolith +1, metal +2, ice / circuit salvage +3, titanium +4, arcadium / rubrium +5
- This stage needs **2 exploration scans** and 30 RP. Then **upgrade to auto-cleaning panels**.
- A **dust storm** hits. Your self-cleaning array keeps charging through it. Survive it to finish the act.

### Act III — Water, Food & Flares
- Ration packs run out, so **hunger** becomes active.
- **Find water**: mine 6 blue water crystals in the ice cavern. Mined crystals re-freeze every dawn.
- **Purify** them at the base: build the purifier, then press E at the dome to turn ice into water.
- **Artificial agriculture**: build the hydroponics bay, plant (1 water), harvest, and eat (Q).
- **Solar flare plans**:
  - *Avoid*: shield the dome with a regolith berm.
  - *Recover*: install surge breakers and backup cells at the array (needs 60 RP).
- The **solar flare** hits. Shelter in the shielded dome or underground. Afterwards, reboot the tripped solar array.

### Act IV — Underground
- Stockpile surface materials. After a sol of cavern-roof monitoring ("after some time"), **build the underground base** in the ice cavern. It's a pressurised shelter, and its drill bores a shaft into the deep strata.
- **Mine a stack of 10 titanium** underground.
- **Enhance the base** with a titanium frame: no pressure leaks, faster oxygen, warmer interior.

### Act V — New Elements
- **Discover the two new elements** in the Deep Hollows: **Arcadium** (cyan, far west) and **Rubrium** (red, far east). You can't mine either crystal until you've analysed its anomaly.
- Mine 5 of each.
- **Enhance the base**: an **Arcadium reactor** in the underground base (needs 120 RP) and a **Rubrium alloy lattice** on the dome.
- **Mission successful: Arcadia subsists.** You can keep exploring afterwards.

## Survival

- **Oxygen** drains outside. It refills in the lander (slowly), the sealed and powered dome, and the underground base.
- **Power** is one shared battery for suit and colony. It's charged by the fuel cell (which runs out), solar (affected by daylight, dust, storms and flares) and, in the end, the reactor.
- **Heat** drops at night and in storms; shelters warm you.
- **Radiation** spikes during the solar flare. A shielded dome or being underground protects you.
- **Nutrition** (Act III and later) drops over time. Eat food from the hydroponics bay.
- If something kills you, you respawn at your best shelter and lose 25% of your common cargo.

## Saves

- Autosaves every 30 seconds, after every completed objective, and every time you rest.
- **Esc → Save / Return to Main Menu** saves and exits. **Continue Expedition** resumes the run.
- Saves from the earlier prototype aren't compatible; Continue starts a new expedition instead.

## Project structure

- `scripts/main.gd`: game loop, survival simulation, input, HUD, saving.
- `scripts/campaign.gd`: **the five-act campaign**: objective steps, sol clock, research points, build costs, dust storm and solar flare events. To tune costs or text, edit the `STEPS` table.
- `scripts/world.gd`: terrain (surface, ice cavern, deep strata, deep hollows), ladders, build sites, exploration zones, discoveries, module art.
- `scripts/player.gd`: engineer movement, ladder climbing, mining.
- `scripts/hud.gd`: the HUD.
- `scripts/colony_art.gd`: structures, markers, weather and all lights.
- `scripts/sky.gd`: sky, sun, stars and Phobos.
- `scripts/main_menu.gd`, `scripts/intro_cutscene.gd`, `scripts/descent_ship.gd`: menus and the opening cinematic.
- `tests/screens.gd`: renders sample screenshots (needs a display):
  `godot --path . -s res://tests/screens.gd -- /output/folder`
- `tests/playtest.gd`: automated playthrough of all five acts. Run it with
  `godot --headless --path . -s res://tests/playtest.gd`
